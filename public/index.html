    HOW THE WAR WORKS

    Each faction is a coin on Solana, and the war runs live off the blockchain.

    Holders are your army. Every holder adds troops to your side of the field, and every new holder sends recruits marching out of the gates.

    Market cap is your strength. The bigger your market cap, the harder your soldiers hit and the longer they last. The side with the bigger market cap holds the field, and at 60% or more it fields its champion.

    Hold the river crossings for extra strength. Breach the enemy gates for glory.
